Freight guides

Road, train, sea or air: how to choose

Road is the most flexible for door-to-door delivery on short and medium distances. Train suits heavy, regular volumes between big hubs and usually costs less per tonne. Sea freight is the cheapest way to move large volumes overseas but takes the longest. Air is the fastest and the most expensive, best for urgent or high-value cargo. Many shipments combine two or more modes.

What is CBM and how do I calculate it?

CBM means cubic metre, the space your cargo takes up. For one box, multiply length x width x height in metres, then multiply by the number of boxes. Carriers often charge by the greater of actual weight and volume weight, so light but bulky cargo can cost more than you expect. Use the calculator above for a quick answer.

Documents you usually need to export from Pakistan

Most export shipments need a commercial invoice, a packing list, a bill of lading or air waybill, and a goods declaration filed with customs. Some products also need a certificate of origin or special permits. Ask us early so the paperwork is ready before the cargo moves.

How to pack cargo to avoid damage

Use strong cartons or crates sized for the goods, fill empty space and keep heavy items at the bottom. Put goods on pallets when possible, wrap them tightly, and label every package with the destination and a contact number. Photograph the packed cargo before it leaves.
